About Healed And Loved Animal Sanctuary (HALAS)

We're dedicated to connecting loving pets with their forever homes and making a difference in animal welfare.

Our Mission

Healed And Loved Animal Sanctuary (HALAS) is a nonprofit animal rescue organization committed to saving lives and finding loving homes for abandoned, neglected, and surrendered pets.

Since our founding in 2006, we've successfully matched thousands of pets with caring families. We believe every animal deserves a second chance and a home filled with love.

Our holistic approach includes rescue, rehabilitation, medical care, training, and family matching to ensure lasting, successful adoptions.
