Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about applications, visits, approvals, and adoption requirements.

What is the adoption process?

The process is application submission, shelter review, a scheduled and completed visit, then approval or rejection. Approval reserves the pet for the selected applicant. The adoption is completed only after the handover is confirmed by shelter staff.

Can I apply without completing my profile?

No. Your profile must be complete before submitting an application so the shelter has enough information to review your home and care plan.

Can I reschedule or cancel a visit?

Yes. Open your appointments page and request a reschedule or cancellation. An admin will approve or reject the request.

When can an application be approved?

An application can only be approved after the visit is marked completed by the shelter. Approval reserves the pet pending handover; it does not complete the adoption.

How do I contact the shelter?

Sign in and use the message page, or open the contact page for help links.
