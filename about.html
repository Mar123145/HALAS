About Healed And Loved Animal Sanctuary (HALAS)

We're dedicated to connecting loving pets with their forever homes and making a difference in animal welfare.

Our Mission

Healed And Loved Animal Sanctuary (HALAS) is committed to animal welfare and to finding loving homes for pets in need.

We believe every animal deserves compassionate care, a second chance, and a home filled with love.

Our holistic approach includes rescue, rehabilitation, medical care, training, and family matching to ensure lasting, successful adoptions.
